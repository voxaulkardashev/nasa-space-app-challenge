# Gale Crater weather on six 2026 dates (not shown on the website)

The team decided the website shows **only live values** for Gale (Compare view → Temperature/Weather). These daily values were read for an earlier version of the page and are kept here for reference.

- **Source:** [NASA Goddard PSG Mars Explorer](https://psg.gsfc.nasa.gov/apps/mars/), read 15 November 2026.
- **Settings:**
  - location 5.37° S, 137.81° E (Gale crater centre, IAU/USGS Gazetteer)
  - model EMARS "Most similar MY"
  - units SI
  - each date at 12:00 UT
- **Values:** from PSG's "Today" row. The air temperature is the daily minimum and maximum; pressure, wind and dust are daily maxima.
- **What they are:** model values, not measurements. PSG has no measured 2026 data. For every date below it reported "Climatological data based on EMARS MY32 Moderate dust", meaning it replays Mars Year 32 (2013–2015) at that date's season and time of day.
- **Caveat:** PSG says the app is "undergoing active development" and "should be only used for testing purposes".

**To check a row:** on PSG's site, set the location and model as above, set the date to the 15th of that month at 12:00 UT, and read the "Today" row. Values for a date in the future may change if PSG updates its model.
